Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 16, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Jumble ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (optional)
  • Gamer tag (optional, required for public games)
  • Authentication credentials (securely hashed)

Game Data

During gameplay, we collect:

  • Words you submit
  • Game scores and statistics
  • Match history
  • Leaderboard rankings (for public games only)

Technical Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Error logs and crash reports

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Jumble game service
  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable multiplayer gameplay and matchmaking
  • Display leaderboards and statistics
  • Improve game performance and fix bugs
  • Send important service announcements
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

  • Public Games: Your gamer tag and scores are visible to other players in public games and on leaderboards
  • Private Games: Your display name is visible only to players in your private games
  • Service Providers: We use third-party services for hosting (Fly.io), analytics, and authentication (Google Sign-In)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights

Third-Party Services

Jumble uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Fly.io: For hosting our servers

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/WSS)
  • Secure password hashing
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request account deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of data collection (by not using the service)
  • Export your game data

Children's Privacy

Jumble is rated 9+ and is not intended for children under 9. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 9. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 9, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
