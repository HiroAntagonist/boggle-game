## Description (4000 characters max)

Find words, compete with friends, and climb the leaderboard in this real-time multiplayer word game.

**HOW TO PLAY**
Jumble is a fast-paced word game where you race against the clock to find as many words as possible in a grid of letters. Words must be at least 3 letters long and form a continuous path through adjacent tiles (including diagonals).

**GAME MODES**
• **Public Games**: Join the global competition! Set your gamer tag and compete against players worldwide on the leaderboard
• **Private Games**: Create custom games with friends using a simple share code. Perfect for family game nights or friendly competitions

**FEATURES**
• Real-time multiplayer - see words as other players find them
• Customizable games - choose board size (4×4 or 5×5) and time limits
• Smart scoring - longer words earn more points using Fibonacci sequence (3-letter=1pt, 4-letter=2pts, 5-letter=3pts, etc.)
• Strike-out rule - words found by multiple players score zero for all (classic Boggle strategy!)
• Global leaderboard - track your ranking among all players
• Detailed statistics - view your win rate, best score, and total games played
• Profile customization - set your display name and gamer tag
• Seamless reconnection - lost connection? Rejoin your active game instantly

**GAME RULES**
• Minimum word length: 3 letters
• Adjacent letters only (horizontal, vertical, diagonal)
• Each tile can only be used once per word
• All words validated against official SOWPODS dictionary (267,000+ words)
• Duplicate words across players are struck out and score zero

**PERFECT FOR**
• Word game enthusiasts
• Fans of Boggle, Scrabble, and word puzzles
• Competitive players who love leaderboards
• Friends and families looking for fun multiplayer games
• Anyone who wants to improve their vocabulary

**WHY JUMBLE?**
Unlike traditional turn-based word games, Jumble lets everyone play simultaneously. The real-time competition adds excitement and strategy - do you go for longer words or submit quickly before others? Every game is a fresh challenge with randomized letter grids.

**PRIVACY & SAFETY**
We take your privacy seriously. Only your chosen gamer tag is visible to other players in public games. Private games show only your display name to invited players. No ads, no tracking. See our full privacy policy at https://boggle-game-ar.fly.dev/privacy
